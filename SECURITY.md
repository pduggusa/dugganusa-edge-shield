# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@dugganusa.com**. Include the affected repository, version or commit, steps to reproduce, and what you were able to see or change. You do not need to confirm the channel first.

Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.

## What happens next

We confirm receipt within two business days, tell you whether we could reproduce it, and keep you updated until it is fixed. Fixes for critical or high issues in this repository get a GitHub Security Advisory with a CWE.

## Safe harbor and disclosure

We authorize good-faith security testing that follows our policy, and we will not take legal action against you for it. By default you may publish 90 days after your report, or sooner once the fix is live.
